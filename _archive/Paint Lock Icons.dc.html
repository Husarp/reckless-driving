PAINT LOCK STATES
Five locked-swatch treatments
All glyphs are 12×12 pixel grids drawn as SVG rects with crisp edges, so they scale like the rest of the art. Amber border = affordable, slate border = can't afford yet. Each row shows the real 28px size; the tile at the right is the same markup at 3×.
1a
PLATE
Bone-white plate, dark lock. Refines the current icon: smaller plate, real keyhole, 1px night inset so it sits on the paint instead of floating.
PAINT
1b
SCRIM
No plate. A night scrim darkens the paint and the lock is an open outline, so the colour reads through the body and keyhole. Cheapest visual weight of the five.
PAINT
1c
CORNER BADGE
Swatch stays a swatch, dimmed to 45%; a 12px lock chip hangs off the corner and takes the state colour (amber affordable, slate not). Best colour readability, most fiddly at 28px.
PAINT
1d
HAZARD TAPE
Roadwork hatching over the paint with a small dark lock plate on top. Loudest of the five and the most on-theme for a driving game.
PAINT
1e
SHACKLE STATE
The glyph carries the state instead of the border: shut grey lock when you can't afford it, sprung amber shackle when you can. Reads at a glance without a colour key.
