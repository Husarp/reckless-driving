SHIELD BUMP — FX LAYERS
Ten independent sprite sheets. No car in any frame — every sheet is a transparent overlay you composite onto whatever vehicle is on screen, so nothing here breaks when the model or paint colour changes. The checkerboard is empty alpha. Dotted marks are anchor guides only, never drawn in engine.

3 · AIR RIPPLE — 4-FRAME LOOP @ 90MS
The "currently invincible" state cue. Three rings alive at once, each spawning at r=4 and dying at r=15, offset a third of a loop apart so one is always fresh. Front arc spans 140°, side arcs are clipped to 50° at 0.8× radius so the car never looks encircled. Brightness steps down with ring age; the oldest is dashed. Anchor is the front bumper centre — the dotted box shows a 14×24 footprint for scale, nothing more.

4 · IMPACT EXPLOSION — 3 VARIANTS, 12 FRAMES @ 45MS
Rebuilt bigger and messier: 64×64 instead of 32, and the fireball is a cluster of eleven overlapping lobes with dithered edges instead of a ring, so no frame reads as a circle and no two frames share a silhouette. Smoke is drawn first as 16–20 growing puffs, then the fire, then the spark rain on top. Particles only, centre-anchored on the contact point — no vehicle art, so it drops on top of any car at any size or colour, and your existing squash-and-darken pass handles the wreck underneath. Debris chunks are neutral grey; tint them from the hit vehicle's own colour at draw time if you want the paint to carry through (the Tweaks swatch previews that). Frames 8–12 are smoke and embers only, so they can keep playing over the wreck after the burst is gone.

8 · SPEED AIR — 2 SHEETS, 4-FRAME SEAMLESS LOOPS @ 60MS
The "going faster" read, with no vehicle in either sheet. Standard practice for pixel racers: the streaks never touch the car — the anchor column stays empty and they run in the gap beside it, they are mostly gaps rather than lines, and they are near-white at low alpha so they read as air and not as paint. Lengths and speeds are mixed so the eye can't lock onto one line, and each loop returns to its start row exactly on frame 4, so it tiles at any speed you play it.

9 · AMBIENT ROAD AIR — 32×32 TILE, 4-FRAME LOOP @ 60MS
Not tied to any vehicle: one tile that repeats across the whole road and scrolls with it, so the air reads as passing everywhere rather than only beside the player. 15 particles per tile — single motes and 2-4px streaks, a third of them warm dust rather than white air. The tile wraps on all four edges and steps 8px per frame, so it returns to frame 0 exactly and never seams against its neighbours. SHEET SUMMARY
Sizes and anchors
Thruster 20×22, anchor top-centre (rear bumper). Guard 20×11, anchor bottom-centre (front bumper). Ripple 40×34, anchor bottom-centre. Explosion 64×64, anchor centre. Tracer 20×44, anchor bottom-centre (muzzle). Tank cue 28×22, anchor bottom-centre. Slipstream 10×32, anchor right column against the flank — nothing is drawn on it; shear haze sits 1px clear, nearest streak 2px clear. Mirrored on the other side. Air wake 24×16, anchor top-centre (rear bumper). Ambient road air 32×32, no anchor — tiles on both axes across the full road.
Stacking
All ten are independent and can overlap freely. The ambient road tile runs underneath everything, always on. Sustained state = thruster loop + ripple loop (or tank cue). A hit spawns one explosion instance; several can run at once during the window without touching the loops.
Draw order
Ripples and thruster below the vehicle sprite, guard below it too so the nose silhouette stays intact, explosions and tracer above everything.
Timing
Guard extend 300ms, loops run until the bar empties, explosion 450ms per instance, guard retract 350ms, tracer 240ms. Impacts never gate the loops or the mode timer.
