CRATE-ONLY CARS
Discovered, not owned
Four garage-tile treatments for a car you have seen but can only win from a crate. Each leads with a crate glyph rather than a plain padlock, so it never reads as “buy me for coins”. Every card shows one owned tile for contrast, two locked tiles at real 88×72 size, and the same locked markup at 2×.
1a
SILHOUETTE + CRATE CHIP
Car flattened to a slate silhouette so the shape still reads, with a crate chip hung off the corner. Quietest of the four; a long garage row still scans as cars.
1b
CRATE PLATE + CAPTION
Silhouette drops to half strength behind a centred crate plate, and a caption strip spells out the source. The only option that needs no tooltip to explain itself.
