1c PERFORMANCE SCORE how the weighting works

Every saved run is worth 5% less than the run one place above it. Your best run counts in full; second place counts 95% as much, third 90%, and so on down the top 100. Performance Score is the sum of those weighted values, so a few great runs beat a long pile of average ones.

weight = 0.95 ^ (place − 1)
