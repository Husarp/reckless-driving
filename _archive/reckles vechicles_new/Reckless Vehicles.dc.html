RECKLESS DRIVING — VEHICLE SHEET
Trucks, cars, oddities
Same art rules as the original sprites: 14px wide, 1px black outline, wheels biting the outline, light side on the left, dark on the right, amber headlights at the front and red at the back. Every vehicle here is drawn nose-up, at native resolution, on one shared shell so they all sit together in traffic.
1a SEMI TRUCK — THE FIX
{{ s.label }}
WHAT WAS WRONG
The old truck put the trailer at the top of the sprite and the cab at the bottom, while every car has its windshield and headlights at the top. Read next to a car, the truck looked like it was rolling backwards down the road.
WHAT CHANGED
Cab moved to the front, trailer trails behind it, with a dark coupling gap between the two so the articulation reads. Added: amber headlights and a grill line on the cab nose, mirrors on the cab instead of mid-body, a centre seam and ribs down the trailer, red lights and a bumper bar at the tail, and three wheel groups — steer axle under the cab, twin bogie under the trailer end.

1d IN TRAFFIC — LIVE
READING AT SPEED
At game scale you mostly read silhouette length and roof colour, so each body has one distinguishing block: the pickup's dark bed, the van's blank flank, the bus's window rows, the tanker's bright cylinder highlight, the flatbed's timber. Cars stay in the original palette; trucks and utility vehicles get muted bodies so player-relevant traffic never competes with the red player car.
SUGGESTED SPAWN WEIGHTS
Sedan, hatchback and coupe carry the bulk of traffic. Van, pickup and box truck are common. Semi, tanker, flatbed and bus stay rare because they block a lane for a long time. Taxi and police read as city dressing, tractor as farmland only, motorbike as a fast weaver, ambulance as the existing event vehicle.
