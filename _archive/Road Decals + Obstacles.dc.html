ROAD SURFACE DECALS  +  STATIC OBSTACLES  · v2
Decals are cosmetic marks with no collision; obstacles are things you die on. Both drawn in the game's own idiom — 1px grid, the asphalt palette from makeAsphalt53 (#5a5f66 base, #63686f / #53585f speck, #44484f lane line), deterministic hash placement so a mark at a given world position always renders the same as it scrolls.
Changed in v2: density halved, so the old LIGHT rate is now the densest setting on offer. Every type now has 3 shape variants and takes a size argument — five for the asphalt patch, all squarish and each a different tone darker than the road. Every type appears in the strips, not only on the sheet. Wreck, debris pile and fallen branch dropped. Cones now span the full lane with a broken-down car ahead of them, plus three biome-specific obstacles. Warning sign redrawn to real proportions.
DECAL LIBRARY  — 4x, VARIANTS SIDE BY SIDE
Each draw function takes (x, y, variant, size). The variant picks the silhouette, the size scales it — so 7 types cover about 25 distinct marks, and no two rocks of the road look alike within a run. Patch edges are straight runs that step at two to four points down each side, and the seam outline is traced from a mask so every variant closes properly, including the L-cut.
ASPHALT PATCH — 5 SHAPES
Left to right: ragged rectangle, stepped rectangle with a mild taper, L-shaped cut around a trench, long strip down a wheel path, and a blocky many-sided cut. All five are squarish now — edges are straight runs that step, not slopes, so they read as sawn asphalt rather than spills. Each is darker than the surrounding road, on its own tone between #464b52 and #50555c, since a fresh patch is newer bitumen. Sizes here run 13–34px.
POTHOLE — 3 SHAPES, 4 SIZES
Round-ish, torn-long, and a cracked cluster of two. Shown from 5 to 13px. Darker core than a manhole and a rim on the top-left only, so shape alone separates the two at speed.
MANHOLE — 3
9px disc with a cross seam, 7px plain disc, and a square inspection cover. Round reads at 1x because nothing else on the road is round.
DRAIN — 3
Long grate, short square grate, kerb inlet. All three sit within 6px of the road edge — a drain mid-lane reads as a mistake.
OIL — 3 SIZES
Drip, pool, long smear. Stacked translucent blacks, no rim and no hard edge — deliberately the softest mark in the set so nobody reads it as something to dodge.
SKID — 3 LENGTHS
Short stab, long straight pair, and a swerve that drifts across the lane. Always 12px apart — one car's track width. Length is a parameter, so a panic stop can be twice a tap.
SCUFFED DASH — 3
A clean dash, one worn thin, and one repainted with a ghost of the old line beside it. A flag on the dash rather than a decal, so the art is the stripe you already draw.
DENSITY  — 2x, ONE FULL 576px ASPHALT CYCLE EACH
Shown over the real repeat length rather than a screen's worth, because at these rates you need the whole cycle to see anything happen. Halved from v1: the densest setting on offer is 1 mark per 190px — roughly one thing in view at a time, never two abreast. Both OCCASIONAL panels are that same rate on different seeds; the point is that the type and shape vary run to run, not that the road is busy.
CLEAN — TODAY
No decals at all. Kept for Coast and City arterials.
RARE — 1 / 480px
About one mark per cycle. You notice it as a detail, never as a texture.
OCCASIONAL — 1 / 190px
The ceiling, and half of what v1 called LIGHT. Three marks per cycle: stepped patch, scuffed dash, torn pothole.
OCCASIONAL — SEED 2
Same rate, different draw: skid pair, manhole, oil pool, edge drain, wheel-path strip patch. This is how the rest of the set gets its turn.
BIOME WEIGHTING

STATIC OBSTACLES  — 5x, ONE LANE OF ROAD
Four kinds, each shown in a single 31px lane with its neighbours' lane lines either side. All fit within one lane, so they can ride the vehicle spawn list at speed 0 and inherit the lane-clearance guarantee unchanged. The breakdown is the global one; the other three are biome-gated the same way NPC types already are.
BREAKDOWN — GLOBAL
Barrier spans the full lane width, cones taper back from it, and a car sits ahead of it with its bonnet up and hazards on. The whole thing says "this lane is shut" without a legend. The car is any body from the existing NPC pool in any paint, so no new car art is needed, and orange on grey is the most legible pairing in the game.
ROCKFALL — MOUNTAIN, QUARRY, CANYON
Four faceted boulders and scree in the grey-rock palette those biomes already use. Each rock is straight-sided with a hard split between its lit and shadowed faces, a bright rim upper-left and a crack down the middle — angular rather than rounded, which is what stops it reading as a pile of sacks.
COW — FARM, MEADOW
Broadside across the lane, which is both what cows do and the widest silhouette available. Reuses sprCow53 from the verge, scaled to the car's footprint.
DEER — FOREST, RIVER, MARSH
Facing the road, antlers up, from sprDeer53. Brown on grey is the weakest contrast of the four, so this one leans hardest on the warning sign.
Animals raise one question the others don't: static, or do they wander a lane's width and give you a moving target? Static is the cheap version and the one drawn here.
WARNING SIGN  — 8x
Rebuilt from the real thing: equilateral, symmetric about its centre column, a red border of even weight on all three sides, yellow field, black bar and dot inside it. 17px wide at 1x, just over half a lane. The old 13px version had uneven slopes and no room under the bar for the dot, which is why its exclamation looked crushed.
17 x 15
Every row is an odd width centred on one column, so both slopes are identical.
CLONE — 1px BORDER
15×15, every width step exactly two rows apart, so each slope is one unbroken staircase with no kink at the base. Red border is a single pixel, leaving more yellow field around the mark.
v1, FOR COMPARISON
13px, uneven slopes, dot fused to the bar.
1x / 2x / 3x
The size it ships at. It survives 1x because border and field differ in value, not only hue.
PRE-WARNING  — 3x
Three frames of one approach, read bottom to top in each panel. The sign is pinned to the top of the obstacle's lane and flashes on the ambulance warning's cadence, then hands off to the obstacle itself as that enters view.
1 — SIGN ONLY
Lane 3 flagged. About 1.2s of warning at cruise, matching the ambulance flash.
2 — OBSTACLE ENTERS
Barrier and cones scroll in under the sign, broken-down car ahead of them. Three lanes still clear.
3 — COMMITTED
Sign gone, player alongside in lane 2, one moving car ahead. The worst case worth testing: obstacle and traffic in view together, one open lane left.
OPEN — NEEDS YOUR CALL
Contact rule. Everything here assumes an obstacle ends the run exactly like traffic. A survivable clip needs a damage state, its own feedback and a tuning pass, and it changes what the sign promises.
Animals. Static, or wandering across a lane? Static is drawn; wandering is a small movement system on top.
Rate default. RARE globally with OCCASIONAL on the rough biomes, or one flat rate everywhere for one less number.
