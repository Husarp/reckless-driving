HIGHWAY RECKLESS DRIVING — DESIGN BRIEF

Achievements Tab — Content List

This is the content for the new Achievements tab (replaces the current inert ACHIEVEMENTS placeholder button on the main menu). It's every achievement/mission idea proposed so far, organized by type. No layout, visual style, or screen structure is specified here on purpose — that's entirely up to you. Rename anything if a better name comes to mind, and treat the exact goal numbers (the "X" in each tiered entry) as placeholders to be tuned, not final.

Some entries are marked NEEDS DEFINITION — the idea is real but the exact trigger condition hasn't been pinned down yet (e.g. "do something with the stock car" was never elaborated). Flag these back rather than guessing at what they mean.

Behavior Rules — how the tab needs to work, not how it needs to look

Three sections: one-time, tiered, and secret. Secret achievements stay hidden (name/description withheld) until unlocked.
Tabs to filter the view: ALL / COMPLETED / UNCOMPLETED.
Tiered achievements show two numbers at once: the highest tier already completed, and the next incomplete tier as current progress. Lower tiers must be cleared in order before a higher one counts.
Unlocking any achievement (one-time, a new tier, or a secret) surfaces on the post-run result screen.
Secret achievements should mostly be fun, low-effort discoveries — not grindy — with a couple of harder ones mixed in.
If a section has too many entries to show comfortably at once, sub-section it (by theme, difficulty, whatever makes sense) rather than one long list.
